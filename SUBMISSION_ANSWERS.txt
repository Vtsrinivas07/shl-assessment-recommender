QUESTION 3: Cold-Start Delay?
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
☑ YES

Explanation:
The deployed API on Render's free tier has a cold-start delay. After 
15 minutes of inactivity, the service spins down to conserve resources. 
The first request after inactivity takes approximately 30-60 seconds 
as the service restarts and loads the FAISS index into memory.

Subsequent requests are fast with typical response times of 2-5 seconds. 
This cold-start behavior is a limitation of the free tier and would not 
occur on paid tiers with always-on instances.

- Max Tokens: 1024
- Performance: 750+ tokens/second

Rationale:
Groq was chosen for 10x faster inference compared to OpenAI, providing 
lower latency for conversational experiences while maintaining strong 
instruction-following capabilities.
